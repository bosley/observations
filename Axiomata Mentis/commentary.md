### Instant savants

I suspect the instant savants forms from a shortcut or repaired structure that by happenstance illuminates connections pre-existing in the mind, but this is at the edge of what I can know for certain because nobody yet has the resolution of understanding of the brain that we have over complex human-crafted objects like computers.

### Thought experiment

At this edge of understanding, the fringe claims that this "self" I've been trying to locate the source of akin to that of a signal in space being received/refined/perfected by being entangled with the matter that is my brain-organ. In this edge understanding, brain damage and repair would be akin to the restructuring of a complex antenna rather than a corruption of a harddrive.

If we take the allowance of thought to entertain the notion that the self is something external to the physical self in this way then we also can't assert with certitude that the other parts of the body aren't also somehow intertwined with this etheric self, and that its just the brain that is the primary instrument involved in this etheric self directing logic and making sense of the environment its in.

At the present time I have my suspicions that this is not the case, but nevertheless it is technically a possiblity given what is known.
