# Observations

These are my own observations, written out as numbered propositions. I am working out what I think by writing it down, and keeping the result in a form I can point back to later. The propositions are claims, but they are also the record of how I got to them. Reading them in order is the process, not a summary of it.

## Method

I start by thinking and writing freely, sometimes by speaking and letting speech-to-text take it down. That raw material gets worked into stories or points, and then ironed out until what remains is the thing I actually observed, stated as plainly as I can. Each proposition should be defensible on its own, and later ones cite earlier ones instead of re-deriving them.

## How to Consume

This tractate is a record of how the world appears from here where I am, not a claim that the world must be this way or an argument that you should see it this way.

The numbered lines are a helpful way to reference likeness or contrast, hoping to build a coherent self-refencing work that paints the picture of reality as I am able to see it.

## On AI

AI is a tool I use. Not as the source of any of this, but as an aide for formatting and argumentation. I sometimes use it to turn speech-to-text into consistent prose, and I use it to argue against my points so I can see where they fail. I do this in software I made for my own work and purposes, called [Insula](https://insulalabs.io). 

The point of this project is to think. Generating the thinking with AI would defeat it.
