## Christianity

Christ is the "ideal man," something that, as has been communicated by Christians, if we "accept" — meaning to follow and live the teachings of, meaning to operate in-experience — and a constant plea to self-reflect and make a variant of my notion of an authentic self. Some variants claim that the Christ is essentially a persona (yes, persona) being stretched out from my Primordiant, through the scales present, into the material, merging as a god-man (large debate on this aspect of Christology, and it has soteriological implications) to teach and inform as well as alleviate sin (missing the mark, similar to my usage of it), so hone the light of self on what the scripture deems important.Ultimately the claim is through this acceptance, and to arguable degrees, that the fathoming mind will begin to fathom again once it passes the singularity of death and reaches "the end". Some argue with aspects of judgement, placing the acceptance of the christ as a sort of adjudicator on your behalf on why youve spent so many fathoms willingly engaging in things you knew were "off the mark". When writing this try to stay sterile and not impose any personal beliefes in verbiage. I have my opinion (which of course im right) but I can not assert the truth within the logic of my tractate, only the framework for determining it after considering how i model my landscapes and they (the reader) live experiences that etch their landscape
