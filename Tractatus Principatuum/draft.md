# 16. Attachment

**16.0** A notion I already associate with the self (6.0) may draw me toward another notion. I will call that draw `attachment`.

**16.01** What I meet in the environment is another mind, or a body, or money, or power, and I take it as wearing that other notion. The wearing can miss (5.0, 7.1).

**16.02** The wearing can be of a mind I could fathom (5.02), or of an aspect, sexual or of money or of power, and with fewer relations in what I take them to be wearing the shape is coarser (12.01). Love, as I mean it here, is the yearning when the other end is held as a mind. Lust is the yearning when the other end is held as an aspect.

**16.03** Familial love is the yearning in 16.02 where the other mind is placed (8.22), and the yearning can be there before an "I" who could have set it (2.2). Fondness and closeness I am taking as that same yearning, coarser or finer by how many relations are in the shape worn (12.01).

**16.1** Hate is the same yearning where the notion associated with the self draws away from the notion worn. It can enhance focus on the subject at hand, and it can show up similar to love. Contempt is attention pushed off so the relation is not made (12.12). The weight can remain after I stop taking them as wearing the notion (9.01).

A yearning toward a form I have seen and not resolved can be there before any notion is associated with the self that could draw toward it (9.02).
