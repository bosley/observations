# 12. Form and Relations

**12.0** A form is the shape outlined by a set of relations. I have not found a form that is one thing sitting alone.

**12.01** The cup being up is that kind of shape. The arm, the muscles, the telling, present together, and none of them is the holding (11.2, 11.21). Take one relation out and the shape I was pointing at is not the shape anymore.

**12.02** A principality is a form of that kind in the soft system. It is bounded enough that I can point at it, and it is made of smaller ones, and it belongs to a larger one (4.31). What accumulates is not a fixed shape but an arrangement that keeps organizing whatever happens next along lines already laid down (4.32).

**12.03** Why this arrangement and not some other is the part 4.32 does not say. Bone was laid down by processes that had mostly finished before the "I" was there (4.01). A principality seems to be laid down the same way, by repetition and consequence, except that the lines are relations, and a relation has two ends.

**12.1** When I observe, or fathom, I am in a relation with what I am observing. The remnant I meet is one end (7.1). The reader I read with is the other (7.2). Observation is that relation, a second reading of a remnant (7.5).

**12.11** The relation does not stay in the material the way the cup stays on the counter. It is recorded, or discovered, or rendered into the same kind of being the mind exists as (1.0). I cannot physically interact with thoughts. I can still find the relation later, as recall, or as imprint, or as a line in the hardened record (1.64, 5.14).

**12.12** A fathom is the span a flame takes, and the flame is attention (10.12, 10.2). Passing a fathom is how a relation can be made. Where attention is, the relation can become. Where attention is pushed off, the relation is not completed (10.22).

**12.13** Without that, a principality is close to any pattern that nests. The emotional landscape is not any pattern (9.1). It is notions existing and relating, and weight is what bends the relations nearest a notion (9.12). The grouping in 11.21 is the same requirement at another scale: a group of things that depend on each other, and nothing in the group is the group.

**12.2** I have not observed a truth I could point at and call the thing itself. What I meet is a remnant across a gap (7.1), read with a reader the remnant had a hand in conditioning (7.2), at a scale I happen to be standing on (7.4). A story can be held up to the hardened record (5.14). The record is still a remnant. 

**12.21** What I can grade is how far the account has come apart from the remnant. Stories may contain some degree of error (5.1). A gap is not, by itself, a lie (5.17). The degrees are degrees of missing the mark (7.0). I will treat what gets called truth as the far end of that grading, the end I do not arrive at. What I actually have are degrees of falsehood.

**12.22** The likeness I want is light. Full light would be the form with no remainder of miss. I do not observe that. I observe illumination that stops, and a shade where it stopped. A silhouette has the outline and none of the substance. Turn the object and the shade turns with it.

**12.23** Evil, as I am using it here, is that shade, not a second kind of being. Suffering is a shade of that shade. The same way a story is not false or true in one stroke, a relation is not good or evil in one stroke. It is how far the "I" stands from the form, and which face of the form the relation is using.

**12.3** The flame consumes fathoms to pass through relations (10.12, 12.12). What the passing shows is the form: the shape the relations outline, which I could not have from one end alone (12.0, 11.21).

**12.31** How I, as "I," relate to that form is the light and the dark of what I construe as good and evil. The construal is still a reading (7.5). It can miss the way any reading misses (7.1–7.4). Weight already bends the ground I read from (9.12). A singularity is where the flame fails to complete and the form will not hold its shape (9.13, 10.22).

**12.32** Happenstance shade is the form left free to be itself, occupying a distance another form cannot take. Fire holds heat at one distance and scorches at another. The physics did not add a rival kind. The cost of a form being a form is that a shape can sit at the wrong distance from another shape.

**12.33** Infliction is the same form turned. Someone faced the lit side and used the unlit side. The shade is still borrowed from the form. What is new is the choosing (8.2). The Primordiant did not add a second implementation to make the turning possible (11.41). It left the form free enough to be faced or turned.

**12.34** A form pressed into brightness with no remainder would not stand as a form. There would be no edge, and no one left at a distance to receive it. I am not settling, here, whether that remainder is good. I am saying the shade is how a form shows, once a flame is in relation with it.

**12.345** Without day I cannot have night. Night is the shade of a light that has gone around, or gone down, and the day is what lets me say where the dark stops. Darkness is the case with no day in it. It has no contour. I cannot walk around it, and I cannot say where it ends. Evil, if it is a shade (12.23), is night, not darkness. Darkness would be no form left to cast one.

# 13. Death

**13.0** Death, as I mean it here, is the body ending. Not every ending. The one where the soft system stops and I do not observe what it was holding coming along with the bone (4.03).

**13.01** I am assuming the fade, the natural one, where the nervous system fails by degrees and the hard part is what stays longest (4.01, 4.03). A death that cuts the body off while the soft system is still registering is a different case. 

**13.1** While the soft system is still registering, "now" is already gone by the time I ask after it (10.0). The "I" I can point at is one I am already remembering (10.01). Death does not introduce that. It is where the remembering runs out of a system that can still host it (1.5).

**13.11** Follow "who am I now?" down and there is no self left to point at (10.1). A fathom is the floor under that, not a fixed size (10.14). In the fade, the floors get fewer. Habit and the impress go on driving a variant of "I" after recall is gone (1.61, 1.62). What I expect, and do not observe from inside, is the last floor, and then no unit a self could be built across (10.13).

**13.12** I will call that last floor the final singularity, only by likeness to 9.13. A singularity is where notions cannot hold their independent shape and the process responsible for the ability to know breaks down (9.131). The fade is that region arrived at by the soft system stopping, rather than by a subject releasing weight (9.01). The self collapses onto the smallest "now" the nervous system can still set (10.12), and then the nervous system is not setting one.

**13.2** Observation does not pass that singularity from inside it. The flame is attention, and attention is a soft-system act (10.2, 4.02). When the soft system is among the first things to stop, the passing is what someone else does, the way I already do not observe what the nervous system was holding coming along with the bone (4.03).

**13.21** What can be passed is the remnant. Bone, in the place of the physical remnant (4.1, 4.2). The hardened record of what was done (5.14, 6.21). The social remnant, if the impress is still driving conduct in other bodies (3.3, 3.4). None of those is the "I" of 10.01. They are what a later flame can fathom (7.1, 12.1).

**13.22** The principality does not die in the same stroke as the body, so far as it was never only the body (4.34, 5.13). What stops is this soft system's organizing of whatever happens next (4.32). What may remain is the arrangement as other principalities still hold it, and the fruit already in their record (5.151, 6.4).

**13.3** Prior to time there is no point left at which a record can be identified (11.12). After the last fathom there is no point left at which this "I" can identify one. I do not know what is there. Another event in the record has not ended the record (11.4). The fade ending this reader's fathoms is not the same claim as the record having no further prior.

**13.31** The Primordiant is what 11.12 and 11.4 keep asking after and do not find in the sequence or in the group (11.41). It is holding every present instant, including, if the holding does not depend on a mind that can fathom, the instant no fathom of mine is in. I assume that only in the way 11.412 assumes a ground. I do not observe the holding from the far side of the last floor.

**13.32** If self is what emerges across a run of fathoms (10.15), then a run that has stopped is not a self I can point at. The infinite now, if I mean by that the present with no further span, is the collapse of that run to a floor and then to no floor. I do not get to stand there and report it. The question takes time to run, and by then the system that would run it is what 4.03 says does not remain.
