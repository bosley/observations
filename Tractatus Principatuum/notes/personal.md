One of my earliest memories is me crying on a couch because my great grandma died and I didn't understand (i was a toddler). my mom tried to comfort me by telling me "we dont know what happens after you die. some people think you get to be in your favorite place, and some people think its like before you were born" she described her favorite place and Im sure that will fuck me up when she eventually passes, but the "before you were born" stuck with me. I remember trying to remember as early as I could ever sense. I have recited some very old memories from before i could talk to my mom as an adult that she confirmed. I am unusually able to be impressed by situational memory, smell and ambiance, a single note of a song, multiple memories in vivid detail flood back in. its exhausting and why i dont like to be around a lot of activity for prolonged periods.
