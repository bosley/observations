Aleister Crowley and Peter J. Carroll talk a lot about the landscape I describe. Their rituals were used to break apart installed social remnants by forcing themselves through taboos. They linked the taboos to forms in their mind that they wanted to etch out. I can say nothing as to the truth of their motivations.

Carroll talks about wearing beliefs like a mask. I take that as filtering the light of the flame so a perspective operates as truth for that span. In the realm of the mind, what gets called truth is a degree of falseness.

Crowley exercised it under "the method of science, the aim of religion." It appears he was using his understanding of the scientific process to see what, if anything, happened when the psyche was subjected to physical settings, modalities, and extremities. On the tractate's scaling, he went to another scale, modeled it in a way that was sensible to him, and cast that model onto reality as a living allegory of a form he wanted. He then put the mind through the allegory, changing the landscape on purpose, to see which higher-order patterns, if any, were real.

What I would count as the remnant is not information arriving in the body because the allegory said it would. It is the trauma used so the pattern sits deeper, and a later now is more likely to frame along relations that can etch the form. I have not contemplated this far enough to say whether a later now that frames along some other relation still counts as the form having been etched, or only as the ground having been weighted. I only have a rough outline. My suspicion is that this is what Hermetics meant by "as within so without" and "as above so below," and I am not sure that reading is not the likeness taken for the thing.
