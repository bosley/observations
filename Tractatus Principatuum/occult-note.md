Aleister Crowley and Peter J. Carroll talk a lot about the landscape I describe. Their rituals were used to break apart installed social remnants by forcing themselves through taboos. They linked the taboos to forms in their mind that they wanted to etch out, but I can say nothing as to the truth of their motivations. Carroll talks about wearing beliefs like a mask. This is akin to filtering the light of your flame to fit a perspective and operate in it as truth, because after all, in the realm of the mind, truth is just a measure of degree of falseness.

Crowley exercised it and conceived of "the aims of religion, the methodology of science," where it appears he was attempting to use his understanding of the scientific process to explore what, if anything, happened when you subjected your psyche to various physical settings, modalities, and extremities, seeing which of the gaps crossed from the set of principalities to reality were real. If we look at the tractate scaling hypothesis, think of it as: he went to a different scale and modeled it a way that was sensible, casting a projection onto reality as a living allegory on what he wanted out of a shape of form. He then subjected his mind to exercising and experiencing the allegory, deliberately changing his internal landscape in an attempt to recognize higher order patterns, and what, if any, were "real," meaning "transferred information from the realm of the mind to the realm of the body." My suspicion is this is what Hermetics meant by "as within so without" and "as above so below."
