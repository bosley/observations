# Todo

Not propositions. A list of what the tractate has not said yet. Cites are numbers already on the page.

## Something versus nothing, left with the reader

Authenticity is whether the story stays close to the record (5.17, 6.1). Mitigation is how much of the shaping goes through unexamined (8.21). Neither says why an authentic self would be better than an inauthentic one.

I am not going to say what is good or bad. That is a repercussion of the something-versus-nothing decision the reader makes. The choice I make does not need to be on this page. If it needs to be known, I can be observed and an inference made (5.03, 5.15).

`meaning.md` 9.2 asks whether the account of something and nothing can still support the claim that anything is important at all. The tractate does not answer that for the reader. What it may still need, before fiction, is the place where that decision sits, so a story can have stakes without my having settled them.

## Choice under conditioning

The page leans on the imprinted, habit-driven, principality-shaped self. 6.16 (the ideal I set if I stop and set it) and 8.2 (I choose, and it is chosen for me, and both are in operation) gesture at agency. They do not say how a choice that is mine survives that much conditioning. Fiction needs persons who can choose. Better to find the hole here than mid-story.

## Myth and symbol

Choronzon (9.131), the Primordiant (11.41), and the angels and demons of nations in the writings are inherited religious and occult words doing philosophical work, case by case. A short section should say why I use them as functional models rather than as metaphysical claims.

That keeps the project from being read as more supernatural-committed than I mean, and it is the license for a mythic register in fiction that does not cross 4.341 or skip the doubt in 5.

## Civilizational-scale principalities

`temperature-of-a-culture.md` and `weather-of-self.md` do this off the page. 9.132 already says the singularity in a single mind, in a culture, in a civilisation, may follow the same geometry. It has not been pulled into numbered propositions. Nations, movements, and institutions as principalities with their own weight and singularities is the fiction ground, so writing it here pays twice.
