Proposed section order
Following your stated sequence:
11. Horizontal and Vertical Causality — generalizing 3/10 (horizontal) and 4.33–4.34 (vertical) from self-scale to unrestricted scope. This is the section that has to explicitly clear 7.4's scale error: 4.341–4.342 already flagged the failure mode (treating cross-scale likeness as readable identity, the occult-correspondence trap) as something the tractate itself warns against. Since you're no longer leaning on an empirical crutch like fine-tuning to justify the jump, the burden shifts entirely onto this section doing that clearing explicitly — one or two propositions distinguishing your use of the vertical/horizontal generalization from 4.341's failure mode, rather than leaving a careful reader to wonder whether you've quietly committed it.
12. Forms — the stable-nature primitive, with the abstraction/implementation ambiguity resolved on the page.
13. The Primordiant — defined as what's required by both axes at once: that which sustains vertically (each instant) and grounds horizontally (the chain doesn't run without something prior to it that isn't itself just one more link).
14. Suffering and Form (from on-suffering) — now cites 12 directly instead of reintroducing the light/shadow apparatus from scratch.


Horizontal causality — this is the axis of succession. It's the chain running backward (and forward) through time: this happened because that happened before it, which happened because of what came before that, all the way back — in your phrasing, "the linear chain of causality reaching back before the Big Bang." It's genealogical. It answers how did we get here. Your tractate already has the personal-scale version of this without naming it: Section 3's remnants (the environment existing before me, things I inherit without having witnessed) and Section 10's opening move ("when is now?" — by the time you catch the moment, it's already past) are both horizontal-axis claims, just not generalized past the individual yet.

Vertical causality — this is not succession at all, it's simultaneous support. It's the question of what is presently holding a given instant together, right now, regardless of how that instant was arrived at historically. Your own phrase for it is "the vertical web of dependencies holding every present instant together." It answers what is sustaining this, not what came before this. The personal-scale version you already have is Section 4's principality structure — layers nested inside layers, nothing sitting outside running the whole thing from a single point, and yet the whole thing holds together right now as a shape (4.33). That's a vertical claim about the nervous system. The move for the new section is to strip out the "nervous system" restriction and ask what's doing that same job at every scale, for everything, at once.
