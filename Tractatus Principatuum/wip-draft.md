# 17. Etch

**17.0** A subject I am engaging can release a response whose magnitude does not correspond to the subject (9.0), and the response can be a body act. A fist into a wall. A sentence said before the "I" set it.

**17.01** The subject is the key, not the source (9.01). At the high end the impress has extended to the representation, and a word or an abstraction is enough to release what the encounter installed (9.03). Rough territory is the region bent toward a high-weight notion (9.12). At the far end the ordinary shape will not hold, and the disproportionate response is what I can meet of that from the outside (9.13).

**17.02** The mind can move the body (1.1). At sufficient magnitude the imprint has already crossed into the hard system, closer to something acted on than something acting, and that response may not be mitigable (9.031). The wall is that face. Speech is the other: the mind using the body to encode information (2.1), released early by weight. Same form, the distance deciding which face I meet (15.21).

**17.1** A relation, once made, does not stay in the material. It can be found later as recall, as imprint, or as a line in the hardened record (12.11). When one end of the relation is the reader itself (12.1, 7.5), the repetition of that relation is already a consequence. It does not have to leave the body and come back through the senses before it can install (9.02).

**17.11** The "I" is both stores at once (1.64, 6.01). A considering that stays inside one fathom revises a reader that was already there (7.2). A considering held across a run of fathoms is the emergence of the self one fathom did not have (10.13, 10.15). The "I" I can point at is already a remembered one (10.01). The relation is part of what the next remembered one is made of. Revision of the reader and emergence of the next "I" are one motion. I had split them.

**17.12** Depth is how far the impress has gone. Shallow, the relation stays in recall, and the outline is coarse (12.01). Deeply enough that the mind is no longer tracking the holding (4.32), it lands as imprint in the reader I next read with (7.2, 7.5). At a further depth it crosses into the body (9.031). Depth is not a permission the will grants (14.01). The bent region is where this is available.

**17.2** Language is already that reader in one of its materials. The ones who taught it created the "I" (3.01), and it was impressed the way habit is imprinted on the body (3.3). Refining it is a standard set where 6.17 would have supplied one (6.16). Using the refinement is the acting on the difference (6.2). An ideal that never enters the record is a story about a self (6.31).

**17.21** Use is the repetition. A refined word, said past the point of tracking, installs in the imprint, and the recalled story of the refinement is not the whole of what the word is now doing (7.3, 6.02). At the depth in 9.031 the same use is a readiness in the body to produce the phrase. Released, it is 9.03. Said again, it is another repetition. The release and the etch are the same motion once the depth is there.

**17.22** The fist is the other face of that motion. The act meets the environment, the body supplies the sensory remainder (1.2), and that remainder is a consequence of the same kind as any witnessed state of affairs (9.02, 9.11). The word does not need the wall. The use is already the consequence.

**17.3** A form is the shape a set of relations outlines (12.0). It is not what forms the recorder. What forms the recorder is the relation repeated across fathoms until something emerges that one fathom did not have (10.15, 12.11). I am not putting the form in the Primordiant's seat (11.41).

**17.31** The stare, held as a likeness (15.0), is the flame kept on a region (10.2, 12.12). What comes back is not the region acquiring an eye. It is the reader finding itself as one end of the relation (12.1), installed to the depth the staying reached (17.12). If the flame fails to finish, what gets installed is whatever the failure repeated (10.22). That is the reconditioning in 9.132 arrived at by staying rather than by induced extremity. I am not saying those are the same method, and the page does not say the induced one works.

**17.4** Thoughts still cannot be physically met (1.0). The abstraction does not reach down and carve. The mind moves the body (1.1), or the relation is rendered into the mind's kind of being and repeated there (12.11), and the will was permitted only to the degree the body and the landscape allowed (14.01). Where the release outran the directing (14.1), the "I" watches the record of an act it did not fully set.
