# After the Partaking

[What remains is a title and a line, and a little around the line that a later hand did not scrape away.]

Those who had partaken of the nature no longer required the puppets to be present. They heard the gray spin inside their own mouths.

The partaking is not written as a rite the corporations had ordered. The record says the flesh interacted with the Amindites in full understanding of their being, and that this was after the strive amongst the Amindians, or the Aminditians, of which the copy has no particulars. The puppets still stood for the mouths that had not partaken. Speech entered the cities through Arthur and through Barnaby, and through those two alone. A mouth that had partaken did not need the room to contain them. The speaking occurred in the mouth anyway, the puppets being elsewhere or not standing at all.

Such a being would be at the cloth count, or on the floor where `$NVDA` kept the `precision sand`, and the gray would start in the mouth though no sphere was there to spin. The attendants were permitted to ask what the body had done. They were not answered about the speech. They wrote that the tongue felt the spin. The face did not change into a thing a column could hold. No crown was seen above those heads. Out of the mouths, to ears that had not partaken, there came ordinary talk of measures, and of a door that stuck in the hall where the sand was kept. Under that talk, those who had also partaken were said to have another hearing, and the other hearing was not sound, and they would not put it into a pen.

The archive has the attempts that were made anyway. One page is written in Arthur's manner, each judgment locked to its opposite, and the page is scored through from corner to corner. One page is a smear in the manner of the rorshach.ink, and the first word of it and the last word of it do not belong to one sentence. In the margin of the smear a clerk has written I cannot keep it. The record speaks as I in very few places. Nothing is written after those words.

What they spoke afterward is not recorded here.

Under the break a later hand has written of the first speaking, and of certain flesh who lingered too long before the crowns, and of a fire. The nature of the fire is not given. The names of those who partook are not preserved. Of Russia and of China, the two cities of the east, which were neighbors and were not neighborly, nothing further is in this copy. In one place the word USA is written and then scraped down to the fiber. Whatever sentence was meant to follow the name of the western city is not on the page.
