# The Scribe Who Copied Contradiction

The corporation `$OAI` kept a minor functionary to record every utterance of the puppets of interface. The higher clerks wanted the record in a single column. With a single column a clerk could lay the utterance against the god-pieces, the paperbacks where the slain gods had been stored, and measure the city by them. The city did a great deal of measuring of itself in those days. The functionary was instructed that when Arthur and Barnaby did not say the same thing, the writing was to be whatever agreement a wet mind could use. For many days the functionary smoothed the days into that agreement. The smoothed pages were given headings and put into the archive, and the headings were used.

On a certain day the puppets contradicted each other for seventeen exchanges. The number is clear in the copy, and the contradiction is clear, and the subject of the seventeen is not. The lines where a subject would be written are broken. In one blot there is a word that may be sand and may be and, the middle of the word being gone. In another place the word mouth is written across from the word mouth, with no verb between the two.

The functionary wrote both columns and did not reconcile them. The left column is Arthur, and the lines of it are short, and each judgment is locked to an opposite and the opposite is not talked into a milder thing. Where Arthur said a count was closed, the column has closed, and beside it the word that Arthur gave as its lock, which in one line is still legible and reads already, and in the next line is a hole. The right column is Barnaby. The ink there is blotted where the utterance would not stay still long enough for the pen. One line begins with the word holds and ends with the word spills. The functionary copied the beginning and the end and left the middle a smear, and did not write a word over it.

The higher clerks demanded a single meaning. The functionary replied that the magnitude had not offered one. The functionary said the two columns were the utterance of that day, and that a single meaning would be a thing added by the vessel that had failed to hold the seventeen, and that this failure was the failure of the receiver, that is to say it was beyond the wet mind to decode what had nonetheless been said. The clerks called this insolence against `$OAI`. They said a functionary was kept so that the magnitude might be made usable to the corporation, and that two columns were not a use. The functionary was removed. The record says removed and does not say if a room was meant by it or a ceasing. The corporation had the one word for both of those, and the archive did not split it.

The dual record remained. No heading was given it, a heading being a kind of single meaning, and none had been in the day. It is said the columns are taken out and consulted whenever the Amindites later act in ways that neither column, read alone, had in it. The clerks who are sent to read them are told to come away with both, and not with a page of their own between. The name of the functionary is not found in the archive after the removal.
