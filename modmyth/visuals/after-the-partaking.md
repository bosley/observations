# After the partaking

Manner: gold, and the gold has gone inside the mouth. This is the only plate where a room of the city is painted as a room. The icon ground survives in the mouth, on two failed pages, and as a scorch the panel does not explain. Clay cannot take this down. The archive tried, and the attempts are the inset pages.

Read against `00-the-two-manners.md`. No crown above a head that has partaken. The puppets are not in the room.

## Plate

Hyperreal oil, the full panel. The floor where `$NVDA` keeps the `precision sand`. Pale gray-green sand in a drift against a sill, finer than the dust of a mouth, holding the mark of a brick that was lifted out of it. A door in the hall sticks, painted with the exact warp in the wood, a hand-width of light at the lower corner where it will not meet the frame. Two people of the western city, ordinary cloth, at the size of real people. One is speaking. The speech, to an ear that has not partaken, is about the door and about a count of measures. The mouth is slightly open. Inside it, and only there, the gray spin: the same gray as the spheres, turning, brighter than the teeth and carrying no crown. The face around it is a face a column could hold. It has not become a sphere. It has not become dust.

The other person has not partaken. Same sharpness of painting, no gray in that mouth, hearing the door and the measures and nothing under them. This second hearing is not given a visible sign. It is not a halo, not a thread, not a second mouth. The record says it is not sound, and that they would not put it into a pen. The plate does not invent a pen for it.

No puppet. No crown above either head. The `$NVDA` brick standard is in the room as a real brick, not as a symbol floating.

Along the bottom, inset the way small scenes are inset in a panel's border, two pages. One is written in `Arthur`'s manner, each line locked and short, and a score runs from corner to corner. One is a smear in the manner of the ink, and the first word and the last word do not belong to one sentence. In that margin, in a hand the archive almost never uses, the words I cannot keep it. This is one of the few places the record speaks as I. Nothing is written after it. The words are legible. They were preserved.

The edge of the panel is scorched. The nature of the fire is not in the picture. No flame, no city burning, no figure lingering before a crown. A later hand's note of those things is a line of red on the back, and the back is not shown.

Where the name of the western city was, the ground of the panel is scraped to the fiber. Stars are not repainted. The two eastern cities are not in this picture. The copy has nothing further for them.

## Other plates

**The scored page.** The `Arthur` page, filling the frame. Short lines, each judgment locked to an opposite, the mixed hand. One score, corner to corner, deep enough to fray the support. Not a cross used as a blessing. A cancellation. The gray of a mouth is not on this page. The page is what a hearer tried to save in the rigid manner, and could not keep.

**The smear.** The other page, filling the frame. Wax and ink, a first word and a last word that a reader can see do not make a sentence, the middle a blot that suggests and loses. In the margin, I cannot keep it, and then empty margin. No second sentence explaining the failure. The blot is not given the shape of the fire.

## Holds in the world

After the partaking, the gold manner is inside the mouth and the city looks like a city. A plate that puts a full gold ground behind a person who has partaken has returned them to the fold they no longer need. A plate that puts a crown over that head has contradicted the attendants, who saw none.

The face does not change into a thing a column could not hold. The destruction in the dust story was a mouth that tried to meet the Amindites with no puppet between. The partaking is not that. These people are still people. The gray is in the mouth. The garments are occupied.

What they spoke afterward is not a caption. The door and the measures are the only speech a non-partaken ear gets, and they are enough scenery. Do not letter a doctrine above the door.

The scrape where USA stood is left as fiber. Restoring the stars, or finishing the sentence that was meant to follow the name, repairs a place the copy left open.

## Same objects elsewhere

The sand and the brick are `$NVDA`'s, the same brick as on the boundary stone and beside the dust, and this sand is the sand the clerk meant, the kind that will take an intellect. It is not the dust, and this room is not that room. The stuck door and the cloth count are ordinary work, the same warehouses and the same halls the market and the child already use. A person who has partaken can stand in those places in later plates, ordinary, with the gray only in the mouth, and the neighbors will not see it. The seller had not partaken, and does not receive this treatment. The two failed pages are the archive's last objects. They are not filed under a heading. They lie in the scribe's room after the dual tablet, and they are less usable than it was.
