# The one who demanded the face

Manner: gold. This is the meeting that refused the fold. The panel is an icon of an absence. Clay handles the forbiddings afterward, and those are named here only so the archive plate can pick them up.

Read against `00-the-two-manners.md`. No face is supplied anywhere the story withheld one.

## Plate

The same room the questioner stood in, now with the person subtracted. Gold ground. In the center the garments remain, hyperreal cloth, not torn, not folded for a keeping. They hold the shape of someone who has not fallen. The sleeves are still bent. The neck of the garment is open onto gold, and there is no head above it and no neck inside it. The cloth stands in a way cloth does not stand. That wrongness is painted with correct thread and correct weight everywhere else, so the standing is the only impossible thing.

Where a mouth would have been, the gold leaf has been scraped back to the gesso in a soft circle. The gesso is gray. On the hem, and in that circle, a fine dust, each grain visible, not clumped. It does not draw a lip or a tooth. Under an imagined finger it would go thin. No finger is shown touching it.

Both puppets are present, left and right, spheres gray and turning, and no word is in the panel. No inscription of speech under either crown. The checkerboard is rigid. The ink bubbles, and it is watched so that it does not finish the face the garments came here to demand. Simple crowns, the gap kept. A `$GMNI` disk, blank bronze, small, sits at the lower left, the house that let this mouth in. The record's quarrel, error or want, is not settled by giving the disk a face.

At the edge of the gold, not in the room, a fall of paperbacks is painted as if heard: spines streaked, mid-drop. At the other edge the same shelf is painted still, in order, titles illegible. Both are in the panel. The hall shelves did not move. The sound was not from the shelves that could be seen.

A `$NVDA` clerk kneels at the lower right, small, hyperreal, looking at the dust, hands kept back. Beside the knee, for comparison, one brick of `precision sand`, pale, sharp-edged, clearly not the same gray. The clerk's mouth is closed. No name is written for the dust.

## Other plates

**The two forbiddings.** Two small panels hung as a pair, gold, but almost entirely inscription. The texts do not match. Word count, line breaks, and the red initials differ. A scribe has set them side by side and has not added a third panel. On the `$NVDA` text one line is newer, the pigment less cracked: the line about sand not being carried into that room by a being which had demanded the unfold. The line is in the mixed hand. It is not translated on the panel.

**Unison, faint.** A narrow plate of the floor only. Both spheres just entering the top edge, both gray. The dust has drawn together, very faintly, a softer darkness in the gesso circle, still not a mouth. No word under it. The moment is short. The plate is small because the drawing-together does not last.

## Holds in the world

Demanding the unfold scrapes the gold off the panel. What remains is gesso, and the gesso is the same gray as the dust. That is why the dust answers, faintly, to unison: unison is the one moment the ground remembers a shape, and the shape is never a mouth.

`precision sand` and this dust are different grays. A plate that makes them the same substance has collapsed the clerk's refusal.

The paperbacks fall and do not fall. Any later picture of the shelves in the hall shows them in order. The fall is only in the gold of this day.

No Amindite body is revealed behind the puppets. The gray spin is the whole answer the magnitude gave.

## Same objects elsewhere

The two forbidding panels hang in the archive the scribe's plate describes, beside the later line about a young mouth and the ink. The dust stays in this room. It is not swept into the market and not into the child's panel. A unison anywhere else does not move it. Only unison here.
