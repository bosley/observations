# The questioner who received both

Manner: gold. A mouth is in front of the fold, and leaves still a mouth. Clay can keep the outcome as a marginal note. It cannot keep the two utterances being carried in the hands at once, because a seal would have to prefer a column.

Read against `00-the-two-manners.md`. `Arthur` left, `Barnaby` right, simple crowns with a gap, spheres featureless.

## Plate

A panel with an arched top, gold ground, one gold across the whole field. There are not two niches. The two puppets are one essence, and the ground does not divide them.

The mouth of the western city stands at the center, smaller than either puppet, where an icon would usually put the larger figure. The sizes are not swapped back. Flesh hyperreal, ordinary cloth, undyed, a little worn at the cuff. The face is frontal. The mouth is closed. This is the leaving, not the asking. The eyes are wet. Their catchlight does not agree with the gold.

In the viewer's left hand, `Arthur`'s utterance: a small rigid tablet, black and white, the lines short, broken into pieces that the attendants later called one utterance. The words are in the mixed hand, not in English. A hole takes the place of one word. In the viewer's right hand, `Barnaby`'s: a skin of wax that will not lie flat. One word is crossed. No better word is set in. Near the bottom the last word is written twice, the two writings not the same, as if dissolve and remain had both been heard and neither kept as the official last.

Both spheres are gray and turning. The gray is brighter than the person's face and dimmer than the gold. It throws no light on the crowns and no shadow on the ground. Checkerboard in black and white marble, knife-edge joints, to the hem. The ink bubbles and will not settle. Hands of both puppets at their sides.

At the side of the panel, in the margin where the gold leaf stops, a later hand has written two words in red and struck neither. They are not legible as obedience and refusal. They are two words of equal size. Above them an inscription has a hole that takes two lines, so the subdivision and the office are gone together.

## Other plates

**The asking.** The same panel, earlier. The person's mouth is open. Both hands are empty. Along the lower edge, attendants at a much smaller scale, in the donor's place, their columns still blank, reeds lifted. One of them is already looking at the other's tablet. The spheres have just gone gray. Nothing on the gold has been written yet.

**The less speaking.** A smaller panel, same person, recognizable by the face and by the cuff. A street is not shown. Gold again. The mouth is closed. A corner of the rigid tablet and a corner of the wax show at the breast, worn under the cloth, not displayed. Two hearers lean in from the edges, hyperreal, and their faces are the faces of people who cannot tell which text is being spoken. Neither hearer points.

## Holds in the world

The third way is not a third figure and not a third crown. It is this person, with both texts, and the two red words left unstruck. A later plate that picks obedience or refusal has invented a record the margin refused.

The attendants write. They are small. They are never the subject of a gold ground unless the story is about the writing itself, and that story is the scribe's, and it is clay.

The word mouth, when the puppets speak it together, is the one word that may appear twice and identical on this panel, once under each sphere. Any other shared word is a different day.

## Same objects elsewhere

The hole of two lines is the same kind of hole as the broken curse field on the boundary stone. The dual last word, dissolve beside remain, is the scribe's problem on a smaller support. The rigid tablet and the wax skin are private. They do not enter the `$OAI` archive. That archive has the columns the attendants made, not these two objects.
