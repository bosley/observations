# on-meaning.md point types

Audit map for the rewrite. These labels are not in the prose.

- **DEFINE** — establishes a term, distinction, or condition the rest of the piece uses. Left flat.
- **ENTAIL** — held to follow from something already in place. Left flat, including logical "must."
- **OFFER** — posed for a check against experience, not settled by deduction. Open posture is in the prose (hedge, question, or first-person self-check).
- **FLAGGED** — resists a single label without a guess that would change force. Left unhedged. See the note, and the flags in `on-meaning-changelog.md`.

Mixed points list each clause. Clause labels are the classification; they are not a split of the numbered point.

## 1. Something versus nothing

- **1.0** DEFINE — something versus nothing as the underlying binary behind all meaning.
- **1.1** DEFINE — what that binary defines: the boundaries of contemplating existence and importance.
- **1.2** DEFINE — names that role as its underlying essence.
- **1.3** ENTAIL — from 1.0–1.2: the binary does not direct, with certainty, what a person will deem important.
- **1.4** ENTAIL — it does inform what they will deem important.
- **1.5** ENTAIL — political and religious outlooks come from that, directly or indirectly.

## 2. No authority on origin

- **2.0** DEFINE — the epistemic condition: nobody has authority on the origin of existence itself.
- **2.1** DEFINE — specialist work stays inside the subset perceptible and understandable to us.
- **2.2** ENTAIL — inside that subset, their systems can uncover insight about particular forms and processes.
- **2.3** ENTAIL — from 2.1–2.2: taking a bottom corner of the frame for the whole picture is absurd. The evaluative word is flat because it is treated as following, not as an open test. If that reading is wrong, hedge by hand; do not treat the flatness as closing it.

## 3. No validatable account

- **3.0** ENTAIL — from 2.0–2.1: no account of origins is validatable.
- **3.1** ENTAIL — from 3.0: that does not permit an opt-out.
- **3.2** ENTAIL — from 3.1: if an understanding sufficient for a worldview is not constructed, the already-adopted worldview forms the implied one.
- **3.3** ENTAIL — names the 3.2 result: origin without consideration. Also a label, but not a new distinction; the fragment was left as a fragment.

## 4. The unexamined default

- **4.0** OFFER — observation of what the unexamined modern default often implies. Signaled by "as I take it."
- **4.1** Mixed, both flat:
  - "It is permitted because it is unfalsifiable." ENTAIL — from 3.0 and 4.0.
  - "Unfalsifiable in that way is a hollow exemption..." DEFINE — names that permission as a hollow exemption (origin with no antecedent, allowed because nothing can check it).
- **4.2** ENTAIL — from 3.0: that assumption is no less valid than any other origin-account.
- **4.3** ENTAIL — if the first occurrence from nothing is granted, further occurrences of the same kind are not automatically off the table.

## 5. Ethics and the uncentered

- **5.0** ENTAIL — a worldview from which ethics can be derived necessarily contends with something versus nothing. "Must" is this necessity, not an address. Left.
- **5.1** DEFINE — establishes "uncentered": downstream opinions where that work is not done.
- **5.2** ENTAIL — from 5.1, with the authority condition in 2.0: uncentered opinion has no authority with respect to oughts.

## 6. Singularities

- **6.0** OFFER — the likeness of those boundaries to singularities. "There are boundaries in every dimension of life" is the frame and was not softened; "as I take it" scopes the likeness only.
- **6.1** DEFINE — what those places are: logic going circular, or contradicting itself. Fragment left as a fragment.
- **6.2** OFFER — feelings at each stage remain valid, and added up are naught. Not deduced from 6.0–6.1. Signaled by "as I take it."
- **6.3** ENTAIL — if the landscape and the self-imposed limits are not known, traps expose the singularities and that exposure justifies a feeling response.
- **6.4** ENTAIL — if the stand is known from something versus nothing, that sort of thing gets easy to hear.

## 7. Facts, feelings, and ideals

- **7.0** DEFINE — an opinion is a mix of facts, feelings, and ideals.
- **7.1** DEFINE — how facts stand in this account: they arrive already spoken for, in the way the words are already used. Later points (8.5 especially) use this as given.
- **7.2** FLAGGED — see flags. Left unhedged.
- **7.3** ENTAIL — from 7.1 and from 7.2 if 7.2 is granted: ordinary information becomes threat or relief according to what the account needs it to be. Depends on the unresolved 7.2. Left flat so the dependency is not silently softened.
- **7.4** FLAGGED — see flags. Left unhedged.

## 8. Opposite extremes

- **8.0** OFFER — scene set for a check, not a finding. Open posture is the shift from command to "can be watched," not a hedge on a result.
- **8.1** OFFER — what that watching shows: not two mild readings, but opposite extremes. Signaled by "as I take it."
- **8.2** ENTAIL — from 8.1: they can point at the same frames and still not mean the same thing by them.
- **8.3** ENTAIL — from 8.1–8.2: they are fighting about what the scene counts as, and the feelings scale to that.
- **8.4** ENTAIL — from 8.3 and from 5.0/6.4: without the root of the ideals, which of those is being done cannot be told.
- **8.5** OFFER — the self-check that the look at the facts is already a use given to them. Signaled by the first-person correction ("I think I am... I am..."), on the pattern of on-self 1.0, not by "it seems." A lexical hedge was withheld because it would soften the correction. See flags.

## 9. Application

- **9.0** DEFINE — once the origin-account is in place, the work is application.
- **9.1** OFFER — test whether the account can bear the oughts already lived by, inside the boundaries acceptable as cornerstone questions. Question-test kept; imperative address removed.
- **9.2** OFFER — the first of those questions, already in question form: whether the account of something and nothing can still support the claim that anything is important at all. Left.

## 10. Choice

- **10.0** ENTAIL — from 2.0 and 3.0: because nobody can say with certainty why we are here, or what anything means, choosing cannot be avoided. Structural condition, not a command.
- **10.1** ENTAIL — from 10.0 and 3.2: to choose not to choose is still a choice; the unexamined account will be supplied.
- **10.2** ENTAIL — from 10.1: the lessening is full; political and cultural systems become one with what is nearest in information, not in space.
- **10.3** Mixed, both flat:
  - "Fusion, not mere governance." DEFINE — names the distinction.
  - "The person is dissolved into the account that already fills the channel they live in." ENTAIL — from 10.2.
- **10.4** OFFER — what remains, once the account is formed, is contemplation of the ideal action in this moment. Parallel to 9.1. Imperative address removed.
- **10.5** FLAGGED — see flags. Left unhedged.
