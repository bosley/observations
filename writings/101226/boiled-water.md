# Water

You take a likeness and play it out in the mind as an allegory, while the body is doing the thing the allegory is about. Then you experience whatever happens. You aren't deciding what it means ahead of time. The allegory gives the mind something to move along, and what comes up while it moves is what you look at.

The water isn't carrying anything into you. You're holding a picture.

1. Sit in the heat. Breathe the way you do for the reflection. Light in, shadow out. Set the tongue.

2. Pour the water onto the hot rocks. Watch it, or feel it if it's dark. The water takes on energy from the rocks. Its motion picks up. It stops being water sitting still and becomes steam moving.

3. Follow the motion out into the room. One drop of steam has motion but no temperature. Temperature is what all of it does together. Picture it spreading, the principality of temperature rising and filling the room until there's nowhere in it the heat isn't.

4. Let the heat reach you. When it hits the skin and the lungs, take it in with the light on the in-breath. The motion that came off the rocks is now coming into you.

5. Picture the subconscious taking in that motion. It gets added to the hand you're holding open. Right or left, whichever one is applied. The hand that was still now has motion behind it.

6. Let the motion pull. You're not digging. The heat is doing the moving, and what moves is the view of yourself that was sitting under the surface. Whatever comes up, you see it the way you see the hand. As a stranger. No reasons.

7. Breathe out the shadow. The heat leaves with it, warmer than it came in. Pour again when you're ready, and play it out again.

If nothing comes up, that's what you experienced. If something comes up that doesn't fit the allegory, don't bend it to fit.

## The brain on the rocks

At one point the rocks weren't rocks. In the mind's eye there was a brain, cut in cross-section, the folds bare. The water poured over the folds the way it pours over the stones. It bubbled the instant it touched, and boiled.

What came off wasn't steam, and it wasn't drops of water. It was fruit. With the right hand open, the fruit was good. With the left hand open, it was rotten. Same pour, same heat, same folds. The hand decided which.

This wasn't planned. The fruit is the same fruit from the reflection, what came of the acts, the thing a stranger can see. It came up out of the folds instead of being placed in the hand.
