Sitting still comfortable, breathing deeply until you can’t anymore hold it a couple seconds really slowly repeat. Focus on the breath the way it feels of the air feels as it goes in the lungs. What is temperature is
Hold no thought. Let the thoughts come to you if you get distracted, forgive yourself purge them continue
Continue one to three minutes. The goal is not to be completely uninterrupted before transition. It’s simply setting the tongue, with enough practice the mind will go quiet, keep one to three minutes, no matter how still you are.
When you’re ready begin to consider the self. Self remember. Who are you and how did you get here? What attributes do you possess, or attributes to not possess. What actions do you partake in that are purely for selfish reasons. These are your left hand clear your mind. Self remember, what actions do you partake in that are purely for selfless reasons?
What are your fruits?

Sit with your back straight now hands that rest at your side are in front. Both hands making a fist put resting comfortably in in front of you.
