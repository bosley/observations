Warmth is a touch sense, yet people say “that just warms my heart”—a linguistic contamination that still expresses the attribute of warm found in love because it feels similar, even if from a different sense. So we have this whole mess and grab bag of words and attributes detected inside our entire environment. Following something like what Jung was thinking, I suspect there are patterns common to the human condition—shared biological makeup—so that as we arise and grow, and if we are “infected” with language, that language (used as a tool to better society) creeps into expressing things felt that aren’t in the physical world, or things that express themselves in the physical world over a sequence of time. ### Forms, then the forces

In the idea that time and space as a substance was suddenly on the scene as a downstream byproduct of the emergence of everything in the face of nothing, and that the forms which precede the arrangements of matter exist as part of the all-thing, then we would expect to see various patterns of these forms in nature. It takes these patterns that emerge that follow laws and rules. But where do these laws and rules come from?

Now consider one of the theories about the Big Bang and how energy works. (I recalled reading something along these lines and flagged it for a search and rewrite.) There is a well-established picture in cosmology and particle physics that, at the highest energies right after the Big Bang, the forces we know were not yet distinct. At the Planck scale (around \(10^{-43}\) seconds), there is speculation of a single unified force including gravity (a Theory of Everything). Gravity is thought to have separated first. Then, in the Grand Unification epoch (roughly \(10^{-36}\) to \(10^{-35}\) seconds), the strong, weak, and electromagnetic forces are theorized to have been unified into one grand unified force (GUT models, such as those based on SU(5) or similar groups). As the universe expanded and cooled, spontaneous symmetry breaking occurred—phase transitions analogous to water freezing or a ferromagnet aligning. The strong force separated (often associated with the inflationary epoch). Later, around \(10^{-12}\) to \(10^{-10}\) seconds, the electroweak force (electromagnetism unified with the weak force, which *has* been experimentally confirmed at high energies) underwent electroweak symmetry breaking via the Higgs mechanism: the Higgs field settled into a non-zero vacuum expectation value, giving particles mass and splitting the electroweak interaction into the distinct electromagnetic and weak forces we observe today. The forces “settled” into the lower-energy configurations we recognize—not quite “energy levels” or a simple local minimum in the everyday sense, but through these successive symmetry-breaking phase transitions as temperature dropped. The energy present at the Big Bang is conserved and redistributed; the unified high-energy state differentiated over time into the backdrop that allowed matter to arrange, the first stars, later stars, and all the elements required to make us here today.

It seems, based on my suspicion, that nature rhymes. And if we look at the hermetic principles—“as above, so below; as below, so above”—this idea of mirroring or mimicry. The idea then that there was, at the very first now, just a set of forms that was the unified everything that could be formed under some imposed rule set of the all, then time emerged from that. And then we see the unified force, but over time it becomes played out as the forces we recognize today, as the forms become played out in time.

### Valentinians, and the stop
