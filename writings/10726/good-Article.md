# Good

What do I even think good is. It's relative. Good for who?

I don't know if a greater good can even be considered real. At least, I don't find the notion respectable. The "greater good" is a signal that evil will be utilized, or ignored, by someone somewhere. That is why, no matter the attained level of intellect of a man, he reactively piecemeals enemies in such a way that they become the perfect enemy. The process and the substance used to construct the piecemeal monsters change, depending on perspectives, but often the construction mirrors an insecurity rooted in the being of the individual.

This emerges on a group scale too. Groups of people, when their identity is unified, self-regulate, and each acts in accordance with the group's ideals and processes. Those can be imprinted on the group identity in a way that affects the individual. So depending on how they see themselves in the groups they choose to associate with, their physical condition and situation may illuminate them to a gap between who they think they are and who they think they should be within that particular group.

The mind cannot settle this delta. Or they "feel" it but cannot see it, and then the attributes and behaviors they perceive themselves lacking are the roots of the insecurity. The insecurity shows when they are subjected to an emotionally turbulent context, where the turbulence is caused from their identity with respect to the context itself.
