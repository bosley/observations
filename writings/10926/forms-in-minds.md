# Forms in minds

Considering that forms held in a human mind are "sets of relations," felt and understood, there is a lot of overlap between "systems and stories" of cultures underneath their language, tuned by their history. These things are important, but the patterns "underneath" the languages are similar, often with large areas of contrast in which no overlap, and some that are 1-1. I look at it as if the concepts are higher dimensional, and all of the humans are seeing parts of it. The part a language brings forward is the part a speaker can point at without extra work.

Quechua grammar requires a speaker to mark whether they saw something, heard it, or inferred it. Witness is built into the sentence. English leaves it optional. I don't speak it. If I retell the Quechua sentence in English and drop the evidential, the report can be passed and the requirement is what got dropped. Aymara puts the past ahead and the future behind, where English does the reverse. Nearly every language speaks of time in spatial terms, and still the direction flips. Korean makes the tight or loose fit of one thing in another the basic spatial contrast, where English asks only whether something is in or on. I can say a book is in a box and not have to say whether the box is holding it tight. I don't speak any of the three.

Forms are to minds what math is to the physical world. Math is abstracted from the regularities of the physical world, set down in notation, and the world then answers to it. Forms work one level in. Force, limit, debt, witness, return. Those relations are abstracted from what minds do and leave behind in speech, ritual, and artifact, and minds then answer to them. A mind is made of forms, and it sits in space. I would not press that likeness into an identity. Treating the two as the same kind of thing commits me to forms being readable the way an equation is readable, and I don't think they are. What I want from it is the direction of the abstraction, and the answering-back.

The overlap comes from two sources. Bodies meet frictions that are alike in kind, such as gravity, hunger, weather, scarcity, and death, so the same relations get worked out again. Debt where someone can owe and not pay. Limit where a body stops. Scarcity where there is not enough, so return shows up whether or not anyone has the word yet. And the biological structures that meet those frictions are alike in kind, so the same shapes are easy to hold. That second source stays a hypothesis. Easy to hold is not the same as forced.

I think the higher forms are not "External" but "deeply internal," to the point where I suspect that some aspects of form come-about specifically because of how certain clusters of our biology interface with and experience the world. Its not scientific yet. I hypothesize some aspects of what gives "the shared human experience" mechanically has in it information by-way of the physical structure that supports "the shared experience" that is "whatever overlap our bodies have as similar that produces the conscious experience, or interfaces with it, however it happens (still unknown)." The shared wetware, through operation, creates similar forms.

For instance a truck, a sedan, and a bulldozer are all "vehicles." Their "experience of existence" are different. Compare two sedans of different make but same model and the engines may vary, but the physics and makeup of those engines are approximately equal. They both have an implementation of an alternator, they both have an implementation of air intake sensors, but driving down the same road they will experience significantly different states with-respect to the data that comes off that engine. If we assume for the thought experiment that humans are like this, and that the data coming off our engines is similar, but separate distinct implementations, then it may not be a leap to assume that activity, and patterns of activities that take place in our brain may not all be observable to us directly, but only through indirection. Further, there may be aspects of our inner psychology based on this engine that exist in a way unreachable by language except through apophatic statements. These things would be nearly impossible for us to regularly discuss. Under the hood we are all similar engines. Its how we relate to the forms in our mind through our bootstrapped sense that gives us the internal "Experience" of driving down the road. The sedan does not settle the wetware suspicion. I don't have a mechanism past it.

There is a second likeness I keep near this and don't want to fuse with the engine. Fractals, and the existence of math as higher order forms that the physical world maps onto. We discovered those forms through analyzing the physical world. Math is to the real world what archetypes are to the mind. If I take that seriously I have committed the reader to this much: the archetype is a form the mind maps onto, the way the physical world maps onto a regularity we later write down as math, and what appears is an image colored by the person it showed up in. Weaker than the engine, which has parts I can point at. This one only says which direction the abstraction runs.

I believe that language itself and the growing of the individual human minds since the dawn of time is made partially evident in the language we use. We use language to impress onto new minds enough information for them to consider themselves a "distinct self," and whatever that means comes loaded with the social and cultural baggage of the language and condition we find ourselves in. The self a child comes to is already cut by the grammar they have to speak. Witness marked or left optional, time ahead or behind, fit tight or loose or only in and on.

Through communication "thunking" there exist only imperfect transmissions of the ideas we hold in mind, so the nature of inner-social forms are different than personal are different than other social forms, but many times they outline a similar shared "thing." Personal form is what I have before I have tried to say it. What a particular group holds after the saying is already loaded with that group's baggage, and another group's outline of the same thing can fail to match. The filled-in experience is not in the transmission. What crosses between minds is the structure. The experience itself stays with the individual alone. Speech, ritual, and artifact are where the structure gets left, and where a later mind can find it and answer to it.

Its interesting that I think it comes from the personification of force and form as a mechanism to relate-to and understand from the human-side to the abstract-side.
