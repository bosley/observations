It is unreasonable to assert that we don't exist in some way. This is the condition of every individual that can read this text. The reader cannot remember before they were born, nor can they recall that which lies ahead. Moments are segments of time sliced by some mechanism perceived as the persistent "now," whose chain of succession holds the very continuity of self that we identify as.

Each "now" that we are subjected to arrives in some context. A task at work, a duty in the home, or that which we are drawn to when our circumstances are relatively relaxed, each like a filter over the "now" being experienced. The context in which we are subjected to the now, and our intentions within it, are what form our set of decisions that we make. Whether or not these decisions are made "freely" is irrelevant; the experience of the individual is "having to choose."

Agency, it seems, is the result of some function of being able to understand the context in which the nows are arriving, specific to the domain, for insight on available actions within the perceived authority of the potential agentic actor in the domain. HR (the lords measure) would say a demonstration of agency is when they (the subject) "take it upon themselves" to "work out a problem they saw coming." When people make moves that are appropriate (usually/ideally) and beneficial to a formal hierarchy, it is said to be acting with agency. By this usage, agency is measured by its usefulness to those who set the terms of the context.

The question then is what is driving the overall succession of nows. This is often put in such grandiose phrases like "what are you doing with your life," as if anyone has a solid target of an end goal or drive that they've deeply considered. Most do not. The succession is produced by the contexts already in place, by the decisions those contexts force, by the habits and constraints that shape which options appear, and by the larger arrangements that keep generating the next set of conditions. To change one's full set of circumstances requires a series of decisions across the total succession of nows they face that will alter them. Each decision is still local—made under whatever filter the present context supplies—but the cumulative result can shift the filters that arrive later.

That is to say, meet every moment with intentionality. You do not first need some large purpose that the whole chain is for. The nows arrive either way. What you can do is notice the context the present one is arriving in, see the actions that are actually available inside the authority you have in that domain, and choose one while knowing the choice will become part of the filter on the next now. Intentionality is not having the end figured out. It is treating the choice you are having to make as something that can change the conditions the later nows show up under. The succession continues regardless. The difference is whether the decisions are only reactions to whatever filter is already there, or moves that alter the filters that come after.
