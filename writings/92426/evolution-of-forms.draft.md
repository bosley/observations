I watched a talk on Julian Jaynes. The claim, as it ran it, is that the people of the Iliad were not conscious the way we are. Volition arrived as voices, heard, taken as commands from the gods. The nearest likeness offered now is schizophrenia: auditory hallucinations of a kind that would later get folded into an interior.

If that is even directionally right, it gives me a way to talk about the early gods. They look like a process that was happening to the self while it observed the environment and tried to understand what it was seeing. Forms were taking shape in brains that did not yet have a language to hold them. Out of necessity those forms had to cross a gap. The body had to be used to vocalize them so another mind could take them up.

Over time, at a certain principality scale, members who identify as such carry the principality's hardened record encoded on them to varying degrees. A collective subconscious, I guess, if that is the right word. At the scale of a tribe, thinking and operating in a protolanguage, they begin to record through external imagery, through story, through allegory and metaphor laid onto the physical stuff around them. You can see it in the early sculptures of the gods, the Venus with no face, huge tits and a fat ass, and at Göbekli Tepe, the early temple in Turkey.

They were encoding forms so the next generation could be taught them. Taught the form realm, or the emotional landscape, which is what I called it. I might change that. "Emotional" carries so much baggage that it may be too far off the material. It is also a decent illustration of how language sneaks in errors: a word a degree off, and someone who thought they understood you walks away with a different object.

The space I mean is one whose smallest unit is a relation. Not a string, the way the physics classroom talks about the smallest unit of material. The smallest unit of the space itself. When a mind sees a subject and an object, or two separate things, it makes a relation between them, and that relation branches into relations of relations, ad infinitum. Most of us, I think, only really operate one or two layers deep. Past that it gets heavy, and for most people it becomes less worthwhile.

I picture the forms as taking some sort of material in that space, emerging as something you can represent in the mind however you happen to think. You analyze the relations and check the degree to which they are true. Nothing is ever truly true. There are failures in the senses, and we could be a brain in a vat, and there is no way to really know. What you can check is a degree of falsity. The more true forms are the ones that can be corroborated between multiple minds, represented similarly, with the lowest degree of falsity. Those are the ones that emerge in a protolanguage tribe, get seen over generations, and get encoded so they can be communicated.

As the principality develops, the group develops at multiple scales: the size of the tribe, the complexity of the inner-tribe relations, and how those relations meet the material goods of the environment. Ritual comes up here, and with it a priestly class. I think they sat where they sat because they were the ones more able to hold several perspectives at once and gauge truth across them. For some genetic reason, able to identify the forms in a more abstract way, and also the ones who drove communication.

If you take the video's likeness at face value, the least "schizophrenic" with the highest intelligence would be that class. They could meet someone in the grip of a hallucination and say: I see what you are seeing, these are driving how you feel, this is what you need to do to be better. Different words, especially in a protolanguage.

Geographically isolated groups, inside the principality of humanity itself, all believed in a god for some reason or another. Someone made the insight, I don't remember who, that there are no atheist tribes. I don't know if that's true. It feels true. I looked once and I don't recall the findings. Either way, from a purely materialist reading, humankind may have reached a level of brain development where these hallucinations showed up as a result of already intense and evolving group dynamics. The environment got more complex, which necessitated communication, which was never really good enough, which is why we are still refining it.

There may have been stages where the refinement met the complexity of a given tribe and they could sit for a while. Then a season changes unexpectedly, or a drought, or another tribe invades, and they are met with an outside force that is not understandable, or at least has some friction in it. That friction inspires growth of the group, or death of the group. Complexity then asks for more accurate, more precise communication, and that falls to the ones best fit to communicate and lead. A leader in this situation, as I see it, is one who understands multiple sub-principalities inside the tribe and can hold them in view at once.

You see a version of this in the writings collected under Hermes Trismegistus, which are the works of a bunch of Egyptian priests. A lot of those texts sit close to what I have been saying, and I have been saying a lot of this because I already knew Hermes, so there is some self-mimicry in it. "As above, so below" and "as within, so without" look to me like they are talking about emergent structure. Internal models of the world. If you can dream it you can create it, and if you cannot create it you create the thing that will help create it, specialized tools and so on. Keep the mind on something long enough and you can direct your will in the environment, which is planning, on more than one level, and does not have to be mystical. Planning the corn because you know a hard season is coming. Planning also so that deep strife from unexpected death does not rock the community in a way that will fracture it. You need mitigation in place: a series of rituals to guide people through the forms, to iron out the emotional landscape, so they do not lose cohesion as a group, and survivability goes up.

I do not like pinning this to schizophrenia. I think the guy is right in what I assume he is saying. The word still brings a connotation of being incorrect. We have this notion, I have heard it enough that it exists, that craziness is close to intelligence, that a genius is a step away from being crazy. What I think is happening is a human being bumping up against the edge of what the current set of minds is able to understand. The forms that person is working in have reached a complexity, or a derivation from the norm, such that the standard mind has a hard time walking through them. Or that mind rests at a plane that happens to illuminate a bunch of forms and relations that were otherwise left in the dark.

Intelligence, at a biological level, seems to me downstream of the ability to maintain what I have called a flame. I don't really like talking like that. The observing presence on a relation, and the ability to map the forms from there. What gets taken for intelligence now is the ability to keep high resolution on a strict subset of forms. I think the thing itself is more a case of breadth and depth. Someone very intelligent might only be a jack of all trades: extreme breadth, little depth in each category of forms and their relations. Take that equivalent intellect as a fluid, put it in a different vessel, and the same amount might fall down a tighter path and go deeper. I honestly think intelligence is roughly equal between people, and it is the vessel that has been shaped, through biology and through interaction with the environment, things etched onto you and things you have etched on yourself, that lets the fluid reach further down, or get drawn up and put across more breadth. I picture it as a blue liquid. The exception everyone likes to bring up, knowing it does not disprove the rule, is the case where someone is mentally handicapped. You broke the vessel and the liquid came out, or the vessel couldn't hold it, whatever picture you need, an edge case.

What interests me about the evolution of language in this is the culture, over generations, in a principality held together by shared identity, developing shared forms and ways of moving through the world, the physical realm and also the world made manifest off our biology. Assume the brain came to be by a process somewhat kind to what we call evolution, and that it is the host of our thoughts. Then it is possible there are forms inherent to the physical makeup, that emerge in each of us as Homo sapiens, in the conscious mind and the subconscious, simply due to structure.

I think about how crystals grow. In chip-making they dip a seed rod into a silicate melt in a big quartz bowl and pull it out slowly, and it crystallizes into the discs they use because those discs have a property they need. Something like that, inside the brain's substrate. Temperature, maybe, or some repeated underlying pattern, fractal or not, a pattern that exists, and when it emerges as something we can work with in a mental state, those forms were already there. I don't know if there is literature on this. I would imagine someone has already posited it, because nothing is ever new.

If that were the case, one of the forms responsible for people tending to be religious, in geographically isolated tribes, could have been part of that underlying hardware. Through something analogous to an upper-level principality, on what we would call evolution, the lines that did not produce this were erased, or otherwise never evolved. Digging into the abstract forms, relating to them, may have been responsible for the invention and refinement of communication. And through that invention and refinement over generations, gods were made, and class hierarchy inside tribes further established and delineated.

This is inference across the record. I find it useful. I also know that I don't know, and we won't know. We might get a good degree of confidence. If the assumption is made and it seems to hold, you can make predictions, and I think that is why this even came about. The better the model, the better the action you can take.
