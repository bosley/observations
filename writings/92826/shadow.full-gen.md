# The shadow

The Envoy can be held as a stencil. The stencil is an outline of the ideal variant of the now-self, cut from the Envoy's standards and from the record those standards would already have left at the present scale. Laid on the current self, the cut throws two remainders. Lack is the part of the outline the present has not filled. Excess is what is already etched in the present and has no room inside the cut. The Jungian shadow, considered with this instrument, is in the excess. The lack is the Envoy still ahead. Treating the lack as shadow makes the stencil a punishment, and the dark too coarse to read.

Jung's shadow is the hidden, mostly inferior personality, the underside of the face being worn. It also holds instincts, realistic reactions, and impulses the conscious attitude had no room for. Persona and shadow are the two records of one face. The face is what other minds are reading, or the outline now being used to do the reading. The shadow is what the soft system has been organizing along lines laid down before that reading was tracking them. The traits in it are relations etched and then left out of the role. Recall tells the story. Imprint drives, and the story often has no account of the imprint (7.3). It is not a second being beside the self. In the tractate, shade is how far the "I" stands from a form, and which face of the form the relation is using (12.23). A form under observation leaves a shade where the illumination stops, and the day is what gives that shade a contour (12.22, 12.341). The stencil is the form. The shadow is the contoured remainder on the now-self.

## The stencil

I take the Envoy as it already stands: the name, the posture, the standards in observable terms, the order of precedence, the next act inside the cone. The stencil is the near edge of that, the ideal variant that could be standing here, not the event horizon and not the old list of attributes to be stuck on. A badge-shaped outline throws almost the whole self outside the cut. Cut from the far horizon, every ordinary day fails it, the excess becomes all of me, and there is nothing left to consider.

I write the outline as conduct. What the ideal variant of the now-self would already have done this week, in the roles I actually occupy, at a stretch a disciplined version of me could cross. Feeling like that variant has no part in the cut. The record I would be willing to show is the cut.

## The two remainders

I lay the outline on the record. The journals, the log, what I did, what recurred. Whatever does not pass through the cut, I sort.

Lack: the standard names a conduct, the record does not show it, and I cannot find that conduct already driving me under another name. Nothing in the landscape corresponds yet. Territory unwitnessed is not sitting there as an empty room (9.11). It goes back to the cone. The next act stays with the Envoy.

Excess: the record, or the affect, or an old entry, shows a pull the outline has no room for, and the pull is already mine. It may be contemptible. It may be a realistic refusal, or a competence the standards were too narrow to include. Left out of the role is the whole of what I am looking for. I do not sort it into worth and shame before I have seen that it is outside the cut and already etched. Worth is a later question (6.13).

The comparison of story to ideal is good at the lack. It files excess as lack, "not yet the Envoy," or as a miss I have already confessed. A confessed miss is in the story. That is the ordinary work of holding the story up to the record (6.12). The shadow is the excess the story still will not own, because owning it would change the outline (7.3). The stencil marks the region. The contents are whatever in that region keeps getting filed as distance, or as a fault already admitted.

## Weight and projection

Weight is how I notice the misfiling. A subject in front of me releases a magnitude the subject, taken alone, does not store. The subject is the key, not the source (9.01). Often the subject is another person. I take them as wearing a notion, and the notion is one the stencil cut off of me. Projection is that wearing, plus the key. A magnitude comes out toward a body that did not store it.

The other place is the return to the journals. I read an old worry with the memory of how it played out. Most of that pass irons the landscape: anxieties that were fear of a result, or lack, and that the later fact has already answered. What I mark here is narrower. A pleasure, a cruelty, a competence, a refusal, that the ideal variant has no room for, and that the later fact shows was mine, and that I still will not copy into the record I use for the Envoy.

I can put a question to the Envoy, with the limit already on that talk. At best the answer is my subconscious reflected from that vantage, and I do not know that the output is valid. The question for the Envoy's own use is what it would do. That question lights the lack. The question for this consideration is what the record shows that the outline cannot say and still be that outline. If the voice is contemptuous, it has become a punishment persona, and I rewrite the posture before I trust anything it calls shadow.

## The record

What turns up is written into the record the Envoy is read against. I do not cut a new hole in the stencil so the excess counts as the ideal. Some of what was left out is a realistic reaction or a capacity the standards had no sentence for. A shade full of those means the cone was drawn too tight, and the Envoy is brittle, and the outline should be widened from the record. Some of it is harm the outline was right to exclude. The exclusion stays. The consideration was that I stop telling the story as if it were not in me.

A month of this that produces only a list of ways I fail the Envoy has been the stencil used as a judge. I cut again from the near edge, the ideal variant of the now-self, and I sort the remainder a second time.
