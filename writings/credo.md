## Emergence

At the time of his emergence into the world, the figure of Christ was a form inside the principality of the Hebrew people. A form is the shape outlined by a set of relations, and this one sat inside an arrangement already producing conduct, language, and remnant before the figure was there to be read. He was a form that principality could hold. The relations were the relations of one people.

## Complexity

The figure of Christ I meet in modernity is much more complex than the figure at that emergence. Complexity, as I am using it, is a count of relations. What has accumulated under the name is further principalities, accounts, and records, each answering mostly to what is nearest it. The form at emergence had the outline one people could carry. The form now has the outline of everything that has since organized itself around the name. More relations, and the resolution is finer, and the thing being resolved has outgrown the form that first appeared inside that one principality.

## Record

I am setting manifestation and historicity aside. I cannot meet the mind, or what the stories are about, directly. What I can inspect is the record of today, what was left where a later mind can still see it. The figure I am speaking of is the one that record shows. The principality that has accumulated around the name is what is in front of me either way.

## Principatus Christi

I will call the shared understanding of Christ in totality Principatus Christi. It is the principality: the whole of what is being held under the name, story and impress together, including the parts that do not agree. A shared form is a form more than one mind can find in nearly the same outline, and this one has been carried far enough that it sits above the body. It is bounded enough that I can point at it. It is made of smaller principalities, and it belongs to whatever larger arrangement a given age has set it inside. What makes it one principality is that nesting. Totality is the superset, the shared understanding in full, and any one account is a member of it.

## Principatus Symbolicus Christi

Inside that totality there is a subset I will call Principatus Symbolicus Christi. It is the part that has manifested its own set of hardened records: scripture, creed, canon, liturgy, the durable remnants a later mind can still hold up to the story being told. A principality's own account of itself carries its assumptions forward undoubted. What answers that account is the hardened record. This subset looks to me like the principality attempting to find an authentic self of itself, run at the scale of the group. Authentic, at that scale, is the story the principality tells of Christ staying close to what the principality has actually produced and left. The symbolic records are the instrument of that comparison, and they are already part of what was produced. The reader was conditioned by what it is reading. The attempt proceeds inside that condition.

## Authentic self

The same subset is meant to direct minds toward an authentic self whose oughts and actions stay in line with the further sub-categories nested under it. At the scale of a mind, an authentic self holds the story it tells up to its own record, sets the order of standards it will be judged by, and acts on the difference. The symbolic principality supplies those standards for the roles it foregrounds, and it supplies them as an order, so that where a mind does not stop and set one, this order is what arrives from the principality nearest it. The sub-categories are the smaller principalities inside the figure: what a member is when that membership is under scrutiny, and what the membership requires of conduct. The direction is toward a self whose record moves on those standards. There is no record yet of a self that has finished the work. The authentic self here, as in the tractate, is the doing, and the principality is trying to keep that doing inside its own outline.

## Ideal man

Self-correction through reflection, the crafting of the authentic self, is done toward the ideal man the figure of Christ represents. The ideal is the order of standards a mind arrives at when it stops and sets them. Here that order is already shaped as a man: a life whose oughts and actions are the measure a member holds their own record up to. Reflection reads what was done. The correction moves the record toward that man. The sub-categories are the relations of that ideal, broken out far enough that a particular act can be weighed against a particular face of him.

## Fruits

It provides frameworks for weighing fruits. A principality is known by what was actually done, weighed against the worldview it claims. The symbolic records are one such framework. They give a mind something durable to weigh a life, a church, or an age against, in place of weighing it only against the story currently being told. The member is one piece of that fruit whenever the membership is legible in what the member is doing. What the framework settles is whether the story and the record have come apart. Worth stays a separate question.

## Allegory

It also provides living allegories for etching members. An allegory is a sequence of relations, and the shape is outlined only after a series of acts, distances, and consequences. Lived, the sequence is put through the body while it is being lived: a confession, a fast, a liturgy, a story told the same way. Repeating a relation already made, across further fathoms, is etch. The outline lands in the emotional landscape as territory the mind can find again, and as habit that recall does not have to keep rebuilding. That impress can refine a self toward the authentic self the principality is pointing at, or install a shape the member did not set. The allegory is still a story, and it may contain error. What I am observing is the use. A sequence, lived, so that members come out etched in the relations the sub-categories already hold.

## Divergence

The primary domain of divergence, so far as I can see, is the varying understandings of how the divine interfaces, how it crosses gaps. A mind meets another mind, and meets what a story is about, only by traversing the gap. The accounts that split are accounts of how the divine makes that crossing. Downstream of the split is how the allegories of the drama ought to be understood, and how they ought to be practiced. The same sequence of acts can be lived under different accounts of what is crossing, and the ought attached to the practice changes with the account. The etch lands either way. The divergence is over what that etch is taken to be doing.

## Refinement

The Reformation, and the wars of survival against competing theologies, turned the principalities inward at every scale. A competing theology that can end the group is friction that forces the speaking to get more precise, and what I see under that pressure is the principality defining itself by way of its members. A mind defines itself by reading its own record and correcting toward a standard it has set. Here the members are that record. They self-refine, and the refining is what structures the principality. Held as an analogy, this is an emergent self-reflection at the scale of the principality: the group doing, together, the comparison a single authentic self does inside one body. What a lot of minds do together when they carry that refining in nearly the same shape is a temperature. This one I will call a temperature of refinement. Once it is there it acts back on the members. What feels ordinary, what gets left as fruit, and what is hard to set down, is the work of defining the principality from the inside by the members who are already its record.

## Luther

I see Martin Luther as the first manifestation of a mind that set a large correction in place simply through the observation of the record. His return to scripture, sola scriptura, used scripture as the record to authenticate against. Anything else he treated as noise. The understanding of authenticity looks to me like the one in the tractate, set now at the scale of the principality. A principality is authentic so far as the story it tells of itself stays close to the hardened record. Luther's notion was that the principality leverages scripture as that source of authenticity and of correctness. What the principality itself was doing he read as the account that had come apart from the record. The correction was of an inauthentic self at the scale of the principality: hold the conduct up to scripture, and move the conduct toward the record.

## Generations

Zwingli is the mind for whom that correction reached the drama itself. The Mass, the images, and the inherited rite came down, and the practice that remained was the sign. Scripture stayed, the record a later mind can still open. The drama was the other remnant: the sequence a body is put through while it is being lived, the repetition by which a relation becomes etch. A member born into the principality arrives already inside it, the original observation already behind them. Coherence across that span is the etch already in them when they arrive. Habit, the relations findable again, the form carried in people who were not there for the correction. It looks to me as if the removal of the drama removed the etching that coherence requires across the generational spans of the principality. The next span inherits the record, and inherits it unread by the body.

What the drama was reduced to is a badge of participation. The etching is no longer in the practice. The member's understanding of the badge is put ahead of the badge, and the badge had been a thing in itself, the sequence with the relations in it. I take this for a sly deception. The principality still wears the mark of membership, and the essence of Principatus Christi that had been preserved across the generational gaps has been removed. Whether the action is growth, loss, or only change I leave unset. That is a reflection against the action, and it belongs to the reader; dont read into my observation a notion of good or bad, weigh that against your undestanding of the world and what is important. let that color your feelings not my words and the suspicions they might carry.
