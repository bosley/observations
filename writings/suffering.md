Its no secret that the problem of evil keeps people up at night once they start taking a sustaining ground seriously. To some its a comfort to treat the whole arrangement as ungrounded, simply because the absence of a root sort of levels the playing field and leaves suffering as a cosmic tragedy rather than something held in place. Its as if they believe that evil is _more_ evil if it has a role in outlining what it means to be good, rather than having it be a sort of cruel machinery that nobody is minding.

The Primordiant is the superset from which abstractions and implementations emerge. It preceded the beginning of time and it is holding every present instant. It sustains forms for the sake of the forms.

When light is cast over an object, a shadow falls as the object is illuminated. The light permits the shadow by withholding illumination. The shadow is not darkness itself. It is a shade defined by the absence of light. Without light there is only darkness, and darkness has no contour. You cannot walk around it. You cannot even say where it ends. The edge appears only once something is lit enough to stand as a shape. A silhouette has the outline of the object and none of its substance. Turn the object and the shade turns with it. Stack forms and the shades stack. The darkness in the picture is borrowed.

Happenstance shade belongs to that same order. It has no meat of its own. It does not arrive out of nowhere as a rival kind of being. People talk as if cruelty were a second implementation sitting on the counter. It is the cup’s own contour where the light stops.

The same contour is what warmth is made of. A form that can hold heat at one distance will scorch at another. A form that can carry a body will close over it. If fire were warm at every range it would not be fire. If water yielded whenever a lung asked it to, it would not be water, and there would be no common field in which two walkers could meet. The cost of sustaining forms for the sake of the forms is that a shape can occupy the wrong distance from another shape. Happenstance shade is suffering that appears because a form was left free to be itself.

Happenstance is not the whole picture. A walker can take the same form and turn it. Fire used as a hearth is still fire. Fire used as a brand is still fire. The physics did not change. The aim did. Infliction is suffering caused through the abuse of forms for the sake of the shade. The shade is still borrowed. What is new is the choosing. Someone faced the lit side of the object and used the unlit side on purpose. The Primordiant did not add a second kind of being to make that possible. It only left the form free enough to be turned.

The origin of the light can move and expand. Where it does not move, room remains for objects to turn. The Primordiant could occupy every remainder. It leaves the remainder so the form can still stand. The more forms are given, and the more those forms are left free to face or to turn, the more shadows can be cast and stacked, some by distance, some by aim.

A form pressed into brightness with no remainder would cease to stand as a form. It would dissolve into the light, and there would be no one left to receive it. The people who want a cosmos with no permitted shade are asking for a cosmos with no walkers in it. Darkness is left in the picture because a shape needs an edge, and an edge is where illumination stops. The ground keeps giving the object its being while the object occupies its place. That is what love of particular forms looks like from inside a world that can actually be walked through.
